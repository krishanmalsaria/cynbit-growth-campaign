Experimentation framework

  • Backlog scored by ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease); run the top test each week.
  • One variable per test, with the hypothesis, metric and stop rule written before launch.
  • Weekly cadence: Mon launch, Thu check guardrails, Sun decide: ship, kill or iterate.
  • Guardrails: CAC no more than 20% above target, unsubscribe rate below 1%.

Deliverable 3

Growth strategy report

Growth hypothesis

If we position BitElevate around project proof instead of certificates and let students invite peers for a mentor-session reward, then more students will sign up and the cost per sign-up will fall, because students already distrust certificate-only courses, and peer recommendation is the most credible channel in college communities.

Rationale

  • Pain-led message: the real fear is “I’ll graduate without a job”, so the copy names it and offers a concrete 4-week fix.
  • Low-friction conversion: free application, one field, one clear action.
  • Built-in virality: each sign-up gets a unique referral link; rewards are non-cash (mentor time), which protects margin.

Risks and next steps

  • Paid traffic may skew low-intent; mitigate with the optional college and year fields and quality checks on the first 100 leads.
  • If CAC exceeds target after week 2, shift budget toward ambassadors and referrals.
  • Scale the winning headline and angle into LinkedIn and Google Search in week 4.
