# Campaign Plan: Cynbit × BitElevate

**Goal:** 1,200 BitElevate student sign-ups in 28 days.
**Audience:** 2nd to 4th year engineering and BCA/BSc students.
**Paid budget (assumed):** ₹40,000, all on Instagram ads. Every other channel is organic.

## Metric goals
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Total sign-ups | 1,200 |
| Paid sign-ups | 500 |
| Organic and referral sign-ups | 700 |
| Paid CAC | ₹80 (40,000 ÷ 500) |
| Blended CAC | ₹33.3 (40,000 ÷ 1,200) |
| Ad CTR | ≥ 1.5% |
| Landing page conversion | ≥ 12% |
| Referral share | ≥ 15% of sign-ups |

**Funnel math:** 500 ÷ 12% ≈ 4,200 clicks; 4,200 ÷ 1.5% ≈ 280,000 impressions; CPC ≈ ₹9.5; CPM ≈ ₹143.

## Acquisition channels
| Channel | Type | Why | Sign-up goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram ads | Paid | Most-used app among students; precise targeting | 500 |
| College clubs and ambassadors | Organic | College-backed message builds trust | 300 |
| LinkedIn posts | Organic | Job-hunting students are active here | 100 |
| WhatsApp and Telegram groups | Organic | Free peer sharing, high credibility | 150 |
| Referral loop | Organic | Invite 3 friends, unlock a free mentor session | 150 |

## Experimentation framework
- Rank test ideas by ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease); run one test at a time.
- Write the hypothesis, metric and stop rule before launch.
- Weekly rhythm: launch Monday, check guardrails Thursday, decide Sunday (ship, kill or iterate).
- Guardrails: CAC no more than 20% above target; unsubscribe rate below 1%.

## A/B test setup
**Test 1: Landing headline**
- A (control): "Your first tech job shouldn't depend on who you know."
- B (variant): "Build 1 real project in 4 weeks. Get hired on proof."
- Primary metric: landing page conversion rate (sign-ups ÷ visitors).
- Sample size: about 1,900 visitors per version (12% baseline, detect lift to 15%, 95% confidence, 80% power).
- Duration: at least 7 days, 50/50 random split, no early stopping.

**Test 2: Ad angle** (career anxiety vs. projects over certificates). Metric: CTR, then CAC.
**Test 3: Form length** (email only vs. email + college + year). Metric: sign-up rate and lead quality.

## Four-week plan
| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Launch Instagram ads and Test 1; onboard college clubs |
| 2 | Read Test 1, ship the winner; start LinkedIn posts and WhatsApp sharing |
| 3 | Launch referral loop; run Test 2 |
| 4 | Move budget to the best channel; run Test 3; final report |
